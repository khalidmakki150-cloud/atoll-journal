Guide · July 2026

Monsoon Season, Explained

Two monsoons run the Maldivian calendar — the drier Iruvai from roughly December to March, and the wetter Hulhangu from May to November. Neither one means the trip is off. Hulhangu brings shorter, heavier showers and better surf on the atolls' western edges, while visibility on the eastern dive sites can actually improve. We plan most land excursions around the Iruvai months, and dive trips around whichever swell we're chasing.
